A 100-word villain monologue for make.fun: a dark lord on the night of the eclipse, with his spies, his generals, his gate, and his plan — who keeps losing the thread of his own speech. The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the pauses and the small deflections (the stammered gate, the forgotten step, the raven that ate the list), never in a plain statement. The sting lands in the last line: his villainy, these days, is mostly waiting.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This writes the monologue to `out/entry.txt` (exactly 100 words, 508 characters — inside the 1–600 character gate; the program self-checks both counts at runtime).
